# Consultant question guide: Order-to-Cash, step 2 "Price the order: discounts and approvals"

Prepared by Priya Raman before the 18 Sep session with Dana Ruiz (Head of Retail Sales).
Format: open questions first, then the specifics I need for each configuration item, then exceptions.

## 1. Open
- Walk me through the last chain order you personally looked at, from the moment it arrived to the moment the price was final.
- Who touches the price on an order? Order desk, rep, you, finance?
- Roughly how many orders a week, and how many of those get a manual discount?

## 2. Price build-up (what I need to confirm against the system)
- What does a chain pay, starting from list? Any automatic discount by customer type?
- Distributors and the marketplace: same logic or different?
- Negotiated prices per product: who maintains them, how long are they valid, what happens when they expire?

## 3. Manual discounts and approvals
- How much can a rep give on their own? Above what does someone approve, and who?
- How does the approval reach you? System, email, phone?
- Is there a hard ceiling? What happens if a rep goes over it?
- When did the current rule come in, and why?

## 4. Promotions, volume, free goods
- When a product is on promotion and the order also qualifies for a volume discount, what does the customer get?
- Any buy-X-get-Y, free cases, samples? How are they entered?
- Anything that changes the price or quantity on a line that nobody enters by hand?

## 5. Exceptions, workarounds, pain
- What goes wrong most often with pricing? What do reps complain about?
- Any pricing that happens outside the system: spreadsheets, emails, credit notes after the fact?
- Anything the system does that nobody can explain?

## 6. Close
- Who else should I talk to about pricing? (finance controller, marketing, order desk lead)
- What would you change if you could?
